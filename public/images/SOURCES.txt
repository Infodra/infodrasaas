Product content
===============
LIST360 Business facts were checked at https://www.list360.in/business on
2026-10-06. Listings, CRM and WhatsApp campaigns are available; SMS and email
campaigns are marked coming soon on the source site.

Infodra CRM remains coming soon. Its planned domain, specified by the site
owner, is https://www.crm.infodrasaas.com. Its extended feature list describes
the planned offering, not a currently deployed product.

InfodraBook is a planned cloud accounting product, marked coming soon. Feature
categories were referenced from https://www.zoho.com/in/books/ on 2026-10-06,
including receivables, payables, inventory, projects, reporting, GST workflows,
banking, multi-currency transactions, collaboration and automation. InfodraBook
uses original marketing text and is not presented as affiliated with Zoho.
Its feature list is a proposed scope, not a claim of implemented functionality
or certified tax compliance. No launch domain was supplied.
The products section presents InfodraBook as a full-width featured card with
Billing & Payments, Accounting & Tax, and Reporting & Collaboration groups.
These groups are the shared source for its complete feature list.
Tailwind's content configuration includes src/lib so product gradient classes
defined in the shared catalog are included in production CSS.

Product names in the footer and contact dropdown come from src/lib/constants.ts.
Demo, sales and early-access links use /contact#contact-form. The existing
contact form submission is a frontend simulation; these changes do not add
email delivery or a backend submission endpoint.

The home and contact pages share a floating WhatsApp CTA using the existing
Infodra contact number (+91 93637 53540). Its attention ring pulses slowly and
respects the visitor's reduced-motion preference.
